Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

The short version: this website does not collect your personal information. There are no accounts, sign-up forms, cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or ads on fatmangames.net.

What this website does not do

  • It does not set cookies or use local storage.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising tools of any kind.
  • It does not load fonts, scripts or other content from third-party services. Everything is served from fatmangames.net.
  • It does not ask for, store, sell or share personal information.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may automatically log basic technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser requests a page, in order to deliver and secure the site. Fat Man Games does not receive or have access to those logs. GitHub's handling of that information is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If you email us

If you choose to email hello@fatmangames.net, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not sell or share it. Ask us at any time to delete your messages and we will.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Our games

This policy covers the fatmangames.net website. Games we release, and the stores that sell them, may have their own privacy notices, which will be linked from each game's page.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
